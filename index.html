About關於我

About me關於我

I am a PhD student in mathematics at National Tsing Hua University. My research focuses on number theory in positive characteristic, especially function field arithmetic, multiple zeta values, and multiple Eisenstein series. 我是國立清華大學數學系博士生，研究方向為正特徵數論，特別是函數體算術、多重 zeta 值與多重 Eisenstein 級數。

My PhD advisor is Professor Chieh-Yu Chang. 我的博士指導教授為 張祈瑜教授。
